# DRILLHOLES Component Model

## Source dataset or system

GSWA’s Company Mineral Drillhole Database (MDHDB) compiles company drillhole and surface sample records from public
[WAMEX](wamex.md) reports.

Drillhole records include collar locations. Depending on the submission, they may also include assays, geological logs
and survey measurements. The
[WA Government MDHDB guide](https://www.wa.gov.au/service/natural-resources/mineral-resources/access-company-mineral-drillhole-and-surface-geochemistry-database)
explains coverage, search and download options.
